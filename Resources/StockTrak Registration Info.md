Throughout the semester you will be participating in a Budgeting Simulation. The cost for this e-simulation is $19.99 and is due upon registration.  You can register for and access the budget game simulation at the following link:  

https://www.stocktrak.com:443/members/register?sessionid=328274Links to an external site.

Note that you only need to select the "Stock-trade Account" option that is $19.99.  You do not have to purchase the optional items.

Through the game, you will learn how to manage cash flow, savings goals, and credit over the course of this class.  The simulation is an 18-month game.  Each week you may play up to 2 months of the game.  By the game completion date, you will have played the game for 18 months.  You must complete the full game.   The course schedule in the syllabus provides a recommendation of months to play in order to complete the “18 months” of the game by the due date.  You may choose to play 2 months in a week that 1 month is suggested, just keep in mind that the maximum you can play per week is 2 months of the simulation and you must play all 18 months of the game by the due date.  You should maintain a log of any notes you wish to make when playing (such as due dates of bills, reminders to save and when, etc.) as well as any takeaways from the mini-lessons within the game.  You can document these takeaways on your weekly personal finance journal if desired.

At the start of the game, you will be a college student with a part-time job.  Your income will vary due to variable hours of work as a student.  After 6 months of play, you will “graduate” and get a full-time job.  Throughout the game you will have to make spending and saving decisions.
