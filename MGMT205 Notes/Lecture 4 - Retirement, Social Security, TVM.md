### Social Security
- It is a MYTH that it wont exist by the time I retire
- Employment taxes are taken out of paychecks, and are saved in a social security fund
	- 6.2% of your pay will go towards this fund, and your employer must also match this percentage to put in
- Birthrate in the US is going to decrease, so people working will also decrease
	- There will be less money to pay out to those who are retired
		- Basic economics
	- There will be more people retiring, so they will not get as much
	- How can we fix this?
		- Increase the tax
		- Decrease the benefits for the retirees
		- Reduce who's eligible for retirement
			- Just delays the inevitable
		- Policies to increase birthrate?
			- Roe v. Wade and its impact?
		- Increase immigration
			- Legal and illegal immigration
				- Different ways to handle illegal immigrants
		- Remove the cap - currently at $176,100
- Self-employed?
	- You have to put both sides of the percentage into the fund
- How to be eligible
	- 40 credits
		- 1 credit per $1810 of income in 2025
		- 4 credits max per year $7240
	- Eligible for retirement at 67
		- Can get a reduced benefit if you start at 62 (possible, but not likely a good choice for many)
	- Retirement benefits are based on earnings during highest 35 years of reported earnings
