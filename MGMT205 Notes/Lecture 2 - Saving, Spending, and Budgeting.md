
> [!question] Why do we save?
> Emergency expenses (car repairs, medical emergencies), long-term goals, vacations, family, retirement.

### Some Stats
- 57% of Americans lack the savings to cover a $1,000 emergency expense
	- 63% of Americans could cover a $400 expense
- 22% of Americans have no emergency savings

### Millionaires - Savings & Spending Habits
- Building wealth DID **NOT** REQUIRE a top 10% income
- Building wealth **DID** REQUIRE discipline, sacrifice, and time

### Biggest Obstacle to Saving
- People judge others based on how they look
- Consequently people send more and save less for social acceptance
- it is much easier to look wealthy than it is to become wealthy


> [!note] A thought
> Easiest way to gain affluence is through being frugal
> - Can be on offense, spending more than average
> - Can be on defense, savings more than average

### Emergency Fund
- Cut back discretionary spending (reduce spending by 10% and re-direct those funds to the emergency fund)
- Wage increases and bonuses - contribute 75% until goal is met
- Tax Refunds, Inheritances, Gifts
- Aim for 3 to 6 months of living expensive is ideal

### Planned Expense Savings
- Purpose of this fund:
	- Fund anticipated expenses
	- Avoid borrowing for these expenses
- Investment Choice
	- Low risk, Liquid (high degree)
- Investment / Savings Vehicle
	- CD
	- High yield money market account
	- Short term investment grade bonds

### Set Budget
- Identify Fixed and Variable Expenses and Needs and Wants
- Set 50/30/20 for your budget
	- Version 1 - 50% necessities, 20% savings, 30% wants
	- Version 2 (Sasmaz') - 50% necessities, 30% savings, 20% wants

### Tips for Savings
- Automate as much as possible
	- Use employer-sponsored retirement plans
	- Automatic transfers
	- Savings Apps
- Put all savings towards a financial goal
- Make cutting expenses a game
	- Clean out the freezer month
	- One year no purchase challenge
	- Make certain months no spending months
- Check the internet!
